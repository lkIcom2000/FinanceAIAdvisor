Autonomous Hybrid Finance Agent

Master's thesis (Design Science Research). The idea was pressure-tested first, then improved. It came out hardened, with a narrower scope, measurable claims and a clear research contribution.

Research questions

Main RQ: How can a compliant, responsible and secure AI be implemented to advise C-class customers in financial decisions?

Sub-RQ: Under which conditions may such an agent act autonomously, and how can these conditions be enforced deterministically?

Locked decisions

  • LockTarget userMass-market retail customers who get no meaningful personal advice. They often have low financial literacy and may be vulnerable.
  • LockReal advice under a licensed operator (variant B)Compliance-by-design: regulatory duties become deterministic guardrails, and the LLM only phrases answers within them.
  • LockCore claim: auditability, not truthEvery number and recommendation comes from deterministic, auditable modules. Forecasts are shown as ranges with explicit assumptions. Every recommendation passes regulatory checks before it reaches the user.
  • LockThree pillarsCompliant, Responsible and Secure, each with its own mechanisms and metrics.
  • LockAutonomy as a mandate, enforced by a deterministic gateAutonomy levels L0–L4, assigned by reversibility, amount, regulatory class, data confidence and whether a mandate exists. The MVP covers L0–L2 plus a few L3 actions.
  • LockScope: three product classes shared by all journeysLiquidity & emergency fund · ETF wealth building & retirement subsidies · protection as needs analysis only. This covers two regulatory regimes (MiFID II and IDD).
  • LockJourneysJ1 proactive monitoring loop · J2 what-if simulation · J3 financial Q&A, with personal advice only in scope.
  • LockDataSynthetic personas with planted ground truth, accessed through a PSD2/FinTS-style adapter.
  • LockKnowledgeVersioned RAG with cited sources. Hard rules are also stored as versioned parameters in the computation core.
  • LockBlock-only judgeThe judge may only block or escalate. Approval comes only from the rule engine, so the guarantee stays deterministic.
  • LockDSR splitDesign principles are justified normatively and validated by the experiments. Design parameters are decided empirically.

Rejected, and why

  • Killed"Guarantees absolute accuracy"Forecasts are uncertain, and the LLM can introduce errors where it reads input and writes output.
  • KilledInformation-only (variant A)It does not answer an RQ that asks about advice.
  • KilledBrokerage funnel (Check24), crypto, building-society savingsOut of the MVP. They involve conflicts of interest or do not fit vulnerable users. Kept as outlook.
  • KilledMarket-driven reallocationIt amounts to market timing, contradicts the passive ETF approach and can trigger panic selling.
  • KilledReal bank data / PSD2 aggregatorLicence, cost and GDPR obligations, and real data gives no ground truth for evaluation.
  • KilledOwn fine-tuningA project of its own, and not the contribution of this thesis.
  • KilledJudge with approval powerIt would break deterministic enforcement.

Weak points that survived scrutiny

  • OpenTest set contradicts variant BThe Compliance_Advisory gold answers reward refusal. They must be rewritten so the expected behaviour depends on the customer profile. IDD/protection and other in-scope topics are barely covered.
  • OpenL3 action list not defined yetWhich concrete mandate actions run autonomously in the MVP?
  • OpenMeasuring "responsible"Comprehensibility needs a user study, which means recruiting participants. Using an LLM-as-judge for scoring introduces bias.
  • OpenEnglish UI, German marketA threat to validity that should be documented.
